About Me

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student at Tennessee Technological University with industry experience in IoT, backend systems, APIs, and artificial intelligence. I have a strong foundation in Data Structures and Algorithms, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and Optical Character Recognition (OCR), with hands-on experience in state-of-the-art architectures like YOLO, Mask R-CNN, Faster R-CNN, U-Net, and DeepLabV3+. I enjoy building data-driven solutions and collaborating across teams to deliver practical solutions for real-world problems. Currently, I work as a Research Assistant in the Department of Computer Science at Tennessee Technological University, developing a multimodal flood early warning system in collaboration with an interdisciplinary research team. Previously, I was a Lead Solution DevOps IoT Cloud Engineer in the Business Division at Grameenphone Ltd., where I developed algorithms for "Alo", a Vehicle Tracking System (VTS), to improve customer report accuracy and IoT-based vehicle tracking solutions.
